Chapter one: Nationality

Nationality is the legal bond that exists between a person and a country, in which the person legally belongs to the State, there are two ways that the constitutions set for a person to get the nationality in Colombia:

1.By birth

A person is Colombian by birth if:
  • They were born in Colombia and at least one parent is Colombian
  • They were born in Colombia and one of their foreign parents was legally domiciled (living permanently) in Colombia at the time of birth
  • They were born in another country but have a Colombian mother or father, and later register at a Colombian consulate or establish their residence in Colombia.

2.By adoption

Some people can become Colombians even if they were not born Colombian, for example:
  • Foreigners who legally obtain Colombian nationality through naturalization.
  • Certain Latin American and Caribbean citizens who meet the legal requirements.
  • Members of Indigenous peoples living across border regions, according to reciprocity agreements established by law.

Chapter Two: Citizenship

While nationality stablishes you legally as a Colombian, it`s the citizenship that allows you to participate in the country`s political life. For instance, People under eigheen years can not vote on presidential elections in Colombia


When does a person become a citizen?

A Colombian becomes a citizen at 18 years old. From that age, they can exercise political rights, such as voting and, if they meet legal requirements, holding public office.


Can citizenship be suspended?

Yes. Citizenship may be suspended in situations established by law, such as through a judicial decision. If the legal reason disappears, citizenship rights can be restored.


Chapter Three: Foreigners

You should pay special attention to this chapter, Kofi!

Foreigners` rights:

Foreigners in Colombia generally enjoy the same civil rights as Colombian citizens. For example, they can:

  • Work (if they meet immigration requirements)
  • Own property
  • Sign legal contracts
  • Access the courts
  • Receive legal protection if they are blackmailed
  • Vote (only in municipal or district elections, and they have to meet some standards and legal requisitesin order to do it)

Chapter Four: Territory

Territory is not only the portion of land representated in a map wich Colombia`s government owns, it also includes water bodies and the airspace. here`s a list of thngs that make part of Colombia`s territory.

  • Its continental land
  • The Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina
  • Islands, islets, keys, banks and other territories that belong to the country
  • The territorial sea
  • The contiguous zone (the part of the ocean that we do not use)
  • The continental shelf
  • The Exclusive Economic Zone (another part of the ocean)
  • The airspace (where satellites are instaled)
  • The subsoil
  • The electromagnetic spectrum, where antennas are set

the constiution states that these spaces belong to the citizens and they may as well trust it to the government, (as we do)
